Full description
See when ChatGPT is working or your answer is ready, with a colored dot on the tab icon in Microsoft Edge.

Work in another tab while ChatGPT generates a response. A small status dot helps you see when to come back:

• Blue: ChatGPT is generating a response.
• Green: a response finished while the chat was out of focus.
• No dot: nothing is running or waiting for you to return.

Return to the chat to clear the green dot. If you are already watching the answer, the dot clears when generation finishes. Clicking Stop clears the indicator without marking the cancelled response as unread. Each ChatGPT tab has its own status.

Make it yours
Choose separate Working and Done colors in the extension’s settings. See the current tab’s status, turn the indicator off, or restore the defaults. The status updates while the settings window stays open.

Local settings and clear privacy
Your colors and on/off preference are saved locally in your browser. The extension does not read message transcripts or prompt input. The publisher receives no conversations, account details, or usage analytics, and operates no extension backend. Public favicon images may be downloaded from permitted ChatGPT image hosts without cookies or a referrer; the image server can see your IP address. See the privacy policy for details.

Good to know
Keep the ChatGPT tab open. Reloading or closing it clears unread status. The indicator does not keep sleeping, discarded, or closed tabs running and does not show desktop notifications. ChatGPT must be accessible; sign in if the site requires it. Changes to ChatGPT’s interface may require an extension update. English and Russian generation-control labels are supported; the settings interface is in English.
